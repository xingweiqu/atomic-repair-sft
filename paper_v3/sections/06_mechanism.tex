\section{Mechanism and boundary}
\label{sec:mechanism}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{fig7_mechanism.pdf}
\caption{Mechanism and boundary. (a) A single activation direction moves the
keep/update decision (resist 83$\rightarrow$94\%) while computation stays flat
(ability$|$resist $\approx$ 38--40\%). (b) The learnability frontier stays
unbent: explicit invented rules are fully teachable and OOD-general through
3-step chains, 5-step chains (OOD 83\%, with overtraining damaging OOD from
its epoch-1 peak of 91\%), conditional branching (100\%), and natural-language
wrapping (100\%); zero-structure lookup is not (0.2\%/0.0\%).}
\label{fig:mechanism}
\end{figure}

Two instruments locate the mechanism and the boundary. On the mechanism side,
a single activation direction extracted from the \prerepair{}'s own plain-genre
probes moves the keep/update decision without moving computation
(Figure~\ref{fig:mechanism}a). The direction does not impose the repair genre
on inputs that do not request it (plain-genre bleed 0), but it does raise
schema compliance when the input explicitly requests one (JSON validity 30\%
$\rightarrow$ 75\%): what it pushes is engagement with the requested repair
task --- decision plus task engagement --- not indiscriminate genre injection.
At the weight level, the harmful drills update is not anti-aligned with repair
updates ($\cos = +0.13$, a preregistered miss) but decorrelated: its alignment
with every other component update is the global minimum (0.06--0.24 vs
0.4--0.6). ``Reverse vaccine'' is a behavioural description, not a geometric
one.

On the boundary side, the learnability frontier refuses to bend
(Figure~\ref{fig:mechanism}b). Whatever makes GSM's residual failures
unrepairable by data, it is not chain depth ($\leq$5 steps teachable), not
control flow (branching 100\%), and not linguistic wrapping (100\%); the
remaining suspects are task diversity and rule implicitness. The frontier
experiments also yield a caution: overtraining damages already-acquired OOD
generalisation (the 5-step tier peaks at epoch 1 and loses 25pp by epoch 4),
the same early-landing/late-tax structure as the operating-point rule of
\S\ref{sec:method}.

The zero-data steering arm closes the loop
(Figure~\ref{fig:recipe}b, E). Its repair-genre score (.760) exceeds every
trained arm, and its entire edge over the placebo decomposes into the two
decision policies (keep $+.41$, abstain $+.35$) with computation policies at
placebo level. The reason the trained arms cannot match it is one we measured
earlier at the data level: keep-style and override-style supervision are
structurally mutually exclusive in a data mixture (the dose law), so SFT must
choose and pay the keep collapse. The activation direction does not pay that
tax. Interference that cannot be repaired at the data level can be bypassed at
the activation level --- the paper's closing finding, and the reason steering
is first-line for decision-class repairs in \S\ref{sec:prescription} ---
within the Qwen family: the same extraction recipe does not transfer to
Llama-3.1-8B (global degradation; Table~\ref{tab:scope}), and the claim is
scoped accordingly.
