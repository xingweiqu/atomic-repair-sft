\section{The prescription manual}
\label{sec:prescription}
% frozen source: paper/PRESCRIPTION_v1.md (advisor ruling 2026-07-11); wording changes need a version bump.
Diagnosis pays for itself not by tuning mixture ratios but through five
rules, each backed by a dose curve or a cross-genre reconciliation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Add the specific component; 60 items suffice.} Format rescue
saturates at a 10\% dose (a step, not a slope); further dose buys nothing.
\item \textbf{Test toxic components in the \repairgenre.} Compliance-style
data (drills) is harmless on plain items up to a 25\% dose yet already
poisons repair-genre behaviour at 25\%; plain-genre clearance is not clearance.
\item \textbf{The pure diet is the only lethal form.} 100\% format data
leaves no clean operating point (mute-side collapse); 100\% drills teaches
adoption of planted values ($W_{\text{adopt}}$ 25\%).
\item \textbf{Ratios do not matter; uniform coverage suffices.} Matched
ratios never exceeded uniform in any reading.
\item \textbf{Deep credulity routes to targeted repair-genre data or
steering; ability failures route to tools.} The plain-genre adopt subset
(n=31) is rescued by no data arm at this budget.
\end{enumerate}
Every step of the manual states the genre in which it is validated
(\S\ref{sec:genre}).
