\section{Claim truth table}
\label{app:truth}
The machine-readable master is \texttt{paper/CLAIM\_TRUTH\_TABLE.md} in the
repository; every body claim, its model$\times$domain cells, seed counts,
evidence files and verdict (replicated / variant / absent / untested /
retired) are enumerated there, and every caption in this paper was audited
against it before submission. Retired rows carry autopsies
(Appendix~\ref{app:retired}).
