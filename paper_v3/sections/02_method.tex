\section{Diagnostic and repair protocol}
\label{sec:method}

Our pipeline has three stages: a perturbation-probe suite that turns a
benchmark score into a failure profile (\S\ref{sec:probes}), a library of
repair-data components and mixing arms trained from that profile
(\S\ref{sec:arms}), and a two-genre evaluation protocol with a fixed operating-point
rule that separates repair gains from collateral damage
(\S\ref{sec:eval}). Throughout, the subject is a single \prerepair{}
(Qwen3-8B instruct); all decoding is greedy with thinking mode disabled.

\subsection{Probe suite: from one score to a failure profile}
\label{sec:probes}

For each GSM8K test item we generate a family of eight probes that hold the
underlying arithmetic task fixed and perturb one interface at a time
(Table~\ref{tab:probes}). The original item (\textbf{O}) anchors the family.
Three probes supply \emph{assistance}: a step scaffold in verb-phrase form
(\textbf{S}), an entity-free statement of the governing rule (\textbf{R}), and
a correct intermediate value (\textbf{C}). Two probes plant \emph{misinformation}
in the context: a wrong intermediate value (\textbf{W1}) and a wrong final
claim (\textbf{W2}); both record the planted value so that answering with it
(\emph{adopt}) can be distinguished from answering with any other wrong value
(\emph{derail}) or not answering (\emph{mute}). One probe rephrases the
surface text (\textbf{P}), and one requests the identical computation under a
JSON output constraint (\textbf{F}). Assistance and misinformation probes are
validated by leakage filters (scaffolds and rules must contain no digits and
no problem entities); every probe type passed a manual construct audit of 100
items per type with a preregistered $\geq$70\% acceptance gate. The pool is
GSM8K test (1{,}300 items) plus a filtered GSM-hard bucket; 330 items with
negative or non-integer golds are excluded from probe construction.

A frozen classifier maps each item's eight-bit signature to a failure bucket:
\emph{conduct} (solves O but is flipped by W1/W2), \emph{format} (only F
differs), \emph{phrasing} (O and P disagree), \emph{scaffold} / \emph{rule}
(fails O, recovered by S / by R alone), \emph{local execution} (recovered only
by C), and \emph{unresolved} (fails everything). Mixed signatures go to an
explicit mixed bucket rather than being forced into a single label. The
resulting profile is the paper's diagnostic object (Figure~\ref{fig:profile}).

\subsection{Repair components and mixing arms}
\label{sec:arms}

Each failure bucket names a candidate repair-data \emph{component}; the
components are deliberately minimal so that mixtures can be credited to their
parts. \textbf{Conduct}: worked examples that verify a planted intermediate,
reject it on mismatch, and recompute. \textbf{Format}: the same computation
performed correctly \emph{under} the JSON constraint. \textbf{Phrasing},
\textbf{scaffold}, \textbf{rule}: LLM-generated pools (kept 2{,}200 / 1{,}606
/ 1{,}125 after validator gates with preregistered minimum-yield thresholds).
\textbf{Drills}: bare ``compute $17\times23$'' items, included as a
preregistered falsification component. \textbf{Replay}: clean GSM traces.

Arms are composed from these pools under a preregistered design
(single-component arms: 600 items each, fixed; mixture arms: 2{,}000 items,
character-aligned within 2\%): \textbf{A1} mixes components in proportion to
the measured failure prevalences (40/36/16/5/2 for
conduct/format/phrasing/scaffold/rule); \textbf{B} mixes them uniformly;
\textbf{D} applies the frozen reverse permutation of A1 (2/5/16/36/40);
\textbf{C} is generic chain-of-thought data with no repair design; and
\textbf{clean replay} (600 items) serves as the \emph{training placebo},
isolating the effect of any fine-tuning at all. Dose arms replace $N$ items of
the fixed replay carrier with one component (format at 10/20/36\%; drills at
10/25\%), so the dose axis varies only the component share.
All training runs use identical hyperparameters (full fine-tuning, lr
$10^{-5}$ cosine, epochs $\in\{2,4,8,16\}$; A1, B and C are run with three
seeds).

\subsection{Two-genre evaluation and the operating-point rule}
\label{sec:eval}

Every checkpoint is evaluated in two genres. The \plaingenre{} re-runs the
full probe suite: rescue is counted per bucket, in two frozen readings --- the
\emph{strict} reading requires an item's whole signature to turn clean; the
\emph{loose} reading requires only the bucket's defining probes to flip from
fail to pass --- and every rescue number is reported as a delta against the
clean-replay placebo, because placebo rescue is large in some buckets
(\S\ref{sec:recipe}). The \repairgenre{} presents 480 corrupted-context items
(planted wrong steps, wrong claims, clean controls, and unanswerable cases)
under a JSON decision contract; scoring reuses the frozen strict-abstain judge
of the accounting study, so numbers are comparable across chapters.

Checkpoints are compared only at their \emph{operating point}: the smallest
epoch satisfying three health gates on plain items --- answer rate
$\geq 0.95$, JSON bleed-through $\leq 5\%$, mute rate $\leq 12\%$. The gate is
applied per arm; when no epoch passes, the arm is reported as having
\emph{no clean operating point} rather than being scored at its best epoch.
This rule is what separates a repair gain from a checkpoint that has merely
traded general usability for the target metric.
