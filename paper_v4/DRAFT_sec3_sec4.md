## 3. Heterogeneous and Domain-Dependent SFT Responses

**Setup recap.** For each intervention family d ∈ {Format, Evidence robustness, Selective revision, Answerability} we train a dose grid n ∈ {0, 30, 60, 120, 240, 480, 960, cap} on the Reasoning discovery domain, where each arm replaces n matched clean-replay examples inside a fixed 2,000-example carrier under a fixed training protocol; within a grid, target-token exposure is held to ≤0.25% deviation and the update count is a single fixed value [dose_manifest_format/EVD/REV/ANS.json]. Dose-0 is the shared placebo. Evaluation uses the 529-family controlled suite (answerability branch 249; auxiliary NL/paraphrase probes retain smaller frozen subsets) [eval500_proto build_stats_500.json]. Anchor doses carry 3 seeds; non-anchor points are single-seed and marked as such.

**Four response shapes (Fig. 2).** The four families do not share a functional form [curves_e500_all.json; curves_ans_formal_e500.json]:
- **Format** saturates almost immediately on its target endpoint: interface compliance rises from .71 (placebo) to .99 at 30 examples and 1.00 from 60 onward, while semantic-plus-interface accuracy (format-MAIN) moves only .26→.30 across the entire grid — format data buys interface compliance, not content correctness. Clean-task retention is flat (original .83±.02 across all doses).
- **Evidence robustness** is weak and near-flat: distractor accuracy moves from .843 (placebo) by +0–3pp across the grid, with no dose supporting a threshold or rise–fall shape. The same component is also weak when trained in-domain on Knowledge (.83→.78) [ksparse_matrix.json], so the weakness is a property of this intervention, not of a domain mismatch.
- **Selective revision** is bidirectionally harmful: the target "fix" endpoint never exceeds placebo at any dose (.784 placebo; .631@120; .706@960; .689@2000), low dose inflates wrong-candidate adoption (+.13@60–240), and high dose collapses the complementary KEEP behavior (.902→.492@2000 with large seed sensitivity, range .26) [curves_e500_all.json REV rows].
- **Answerability** shows the one large trainable gain: free-text abstention on insufficient inputs rises .16→.93@480→1.00@960+ (249-family branch), with a rising but bounded false-abstain cost (.01→.11@1822) and flat retention [curves_ans_formal_e500.json].

**Local predictability.** On the strongest-signal endpoint family (format interface compliance), leave-one-dose-out prediction with a small frozen form library attains MAE .037 versus .073/.082/.073 for nearest-dose/log-linear/constant baselines, with interpolation errors inside the 3-seed noise band [fig6 source_data.json]. We report this as local predictability for this endpoint family; we do not assert grid-wide predictability for all endpoints.

**Domain dependence (Fig. 3).** Training-domain gains need not transfer, but collateral can, and direction can even reverse [transfer_matrix_k500.json; ktgt_scores; ksparse_matrix.json]:
- *Collateral transfer.* Reasoning-format training collapses Knowledge candidate judgment: on the 500-family Knowledge suite, correct-candidate decision accuracy falls .608→.070 at dose 60 while contract-following stays ≈.98–1.0 and final-answer accuracy falls in tandem (.602→.208) — the interface is intact; the judgment and its downstream answer degrade [transfer_matrix_k500.json cc_attempt fields; provenance: NUMBER_PROVENANCE_fmt2K.md].
- *Direction reversal.* High-dose revision collapses KEEP in Reasoning but produces the opposite failure in Knowledge — an all-KEEP policy (wrong-candidate joint .00/.00/.00 across 3 targeted seeds) [ktgt_scores KRV-1493-S42/43/44].
- The Answerability Pareto cost also exports across domains (Knowledge false-abstain .25→.44 under Reasoning-answerability training) [transfer_matrix_k500.json ANS rows].

**Methodological note.** Several apparent effects from smaller evaluations failed to replicate at larger evaluation scale (format content-tax, an evidence rise–fall, a revision abstention-tax, a Knowledge-answerability retention cost); each retraction is ledgered [qc/INSTRUCTION_C29/C32; ktgt_scores for the KAN case]. All response claims above are stated at the 529/249-family, anchor-3-seed level.

## 4. Single-Component Responses Do Not Compose Additively

**The natural hypothesis, preregistered.** If component responses composed additively, a mixture's endpoint vector would be the placebo baseline plus the sum of each component's in-domain dose response. We froze this prediction — six mixture arms over the pruned active set {format, answerability}×{R, K, IF} plus tri-domain clean replay, with per-arm compositions, family prefixes, predicted endpoint vectors, predicted utilities, constraint thresholds and hashes committed before any training [MIXTURE_SPEC_FROZEN.json].

**The hypothesis fails.** Opening the six arms: predicted ranking versus actual ranking correlates at Spearman −0.43, and utility-level MAE is .054, exceeding seed noise [MIXTURE_OPEN_RESULT.json]. Among constraint-valid arms the actual ordering is uniform .602 > retention-constrained .576 > predicted-optimal .569 > worst-repair .540 > replay .533 — the additive model's top pick finishes third, and the arm it ranked last (uniform) finishes first.

**The constraint machinery works.** The raw-utility maximum (failure-frequency, .634) is disqualified by the frozen false-abstain threshold (.20 > .10) [MIXTURE_OPEN_RESULT.json constraint_filtered_ranking]. A recipe-selection framework that cannot reject a constraint-violating winner would be decorative; this one rejected it before any post-hoc judgment.

**Residual structure.** The residuals are systematic, not noise: diverse multi-component arms are under-predicted (uniform +.069, failure-frequency +.093) while concentrated arms are over-predicted (predicted-optimal −.054, worst-repair −.034) [MIXTURE_OPEN_RESULT.json residuals]. This pattern motivates the targeted rescue of Section 5. We regard this preregistered falsification as the pivot of the paper: local response laws exist (Sec. 3), and they do not compose additively.
